## Broken: DO_EXPLORATION — `20261115-224622-22b8dq`

The operator reports the bot opens Exploration stage 158, taps Explore, then
leaves Squad Settings with visibly enabled **Quick Deploy** and **Fight**
without executing combat, returning a false scheduler `SUCCEEDED`.

- `017-exploration_button-hit.jpg`: Explore CTA at 98.31%.
- `026-serve_emu_rewards_banner-miss.jpg`: *Squad Settings* with blue Quick
  Deploy and green Fight, neither tapped.
- Log `20261115T224700Z-3cda1aafd920.json`: `Refusing to confirm visually
  similar modal; Quick Exploration text not verified (OCR length=25)` followed
  by `Explore opened an unverified confirmation; no battle input sent`.
- **Root cause:** the generic orange/blue paired-row detector mistook hero
  cards at y≈1025 for a Quick Exploration Cancel/Confirm dialog. It then ran
  OCR on the wrong area, treated the result as an unresolved modal and aborted.
  The scheduler remained `SUCCEEDED` because the early return did not mark
  unfinished work on `DelayedTask`.

**Fix**: `ExplorationSquadFrame.isSquadReady()` requires *Squad Settings*
(99.25%) and the Fight CTA (99.81%) on the **same** 720×1280 screenshot.
Before expensive modal OCR, the routine skips the modal detector on this
confirmed game screen. An additional modal-button geometry check rejects
spurious hero-card pairs below y=74% of the screen; the real modal Confirm is
around y=820 (64%), as proven by existing fixture tests. Existing two-frame
Fight and Quick Deploy verification stays in force for actual clicks. All
unverified branches now `markWorkIncomplete(...)`, making TaskQueue classify
as **DEFERRED**, not **SUCCEEDED**, while rescheduling appropriately.

**Still required**: updated Windows live run to confirm that Fight starts,
actual Victory/Defeat is observed, and Home recovery works. Do not close
Exploration QA or claim it fixed based only on fixture tests.
